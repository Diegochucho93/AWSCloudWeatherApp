# Weather Video Backgrounds - Veo 3 Generation

## 🎬 Overview

This project features 12 custom AI-generated weather background videos created using **Google Veo 3**. These videos provide dynamic, atmospheric backgrounds that change based on current weather conditions and time of day.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### Unified Aesthetic
All videos share a common design approach:
- **Window View Perspective:** Close-up view through a window pane
- **Focus on Glass:** Sharp focus on the window surface and droplets/frost
- **Blurred Background:** Park scene intentionally out-of-focus (bokeh effect)
- **Static Camera:** No camera movement, panning, or zooming
- **Seamless Looping:** Designed for continuous, unnoticeable loops
- **Minimal Motion:** Subtle, natural movements only (swaying trees, falling precipitation)

### Technical Requirements
- **Resolution:** Optimized for web playback
- **Duration:** ~10-15 seconds per loop
- **Compression:** Balanced for quality and file size
- **Format:** MP4 with H.264 codec
- **Consistency:** Uniform lighting and motion patterns for smooth loops

## 🎯 Prompt Engineering Highlights

### Key Elements for Success
1. **Repetition of "consistent" and "steady"** - Ensures seamless looping
2. **"Intentionally out of focus"** - Maintains depth hierarchy
3. **"Camera remains completely static"** - Prevents disorienting movement
4. **"Blurred background"** - Creates depth without distraction
5. **Specific color palettes** - Ensures mood consistency
6. **"No people, no sudden changes"** - Avoids loop discontinuities
7. **Regular intervals for periodic events** - Makes lightning/effects predictable
8. **Ambient sound descriptions** - Helps Veo generate appropriate atmosphere

### Common Patterns Across All Prompts
- Close-up window view perspective
- Focus on glass surface (foreground)
- Blurred park scene (background)
- Static camera
- Consistent lighting
- Minimal, natural movement
- Seamless loop-friendly instructions
- Atmospheric audio cues

## 🎓 What I Learned About AI Video Generation

### Successful Techniques
- ✅ Extreme detail in lighting descriptions
- ✅ Repetition of key requirements ("consistent", "steady", "static")
- ✅ Negative prompts at the end ("No people, no changes")
- ✅ Specific color palette descriptions
- ✅ Focus hierarchy (foreground sharp, background blurred)
- ✅ Audio atmosphere descriptions

### Challenges Overcome
- 🎯 Preventing camera movement (required explicit "static" instruction)
- 🎯 Achieving seamless loops (needed predictable, cyclical motion)
- 🎯 Maintaining focus hierarchy (emphasized "intentionally out of focus")
- 🎯 Consistent lighting across variants (detailed atmosphere descriptions)

### Veo 3 Strengths
- ✨ Excellent bokeh and depth-of-field effects
- ✨ Natural lighting simulation
- ✨ Realistic water droplet physics
- ✨ Atmospheric effects (fog, snow, rain)
- ✨ Seamless looping when properly prompted
